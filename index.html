Helper Goblin

Level up your D&D 5.5e sessions with the ultimate player companion web app. In this application, we offer many aids to help speed up your gameplay experience.

Right now, the content we offer includes the entire 5.5e Basic Rules under SRD 5.2.1. This covers all the basic rules a player may need in a standard game. These rules span from weapon masteries to adventuring gear and even conditions.

There is also the list of spells. Dungeons & Dragons is known for its plethora of spells, so it was only right to add those first. Every spell under SRD 5.2.1 is currently on our website.

Lastly, we provide a dice roller in case players wish to roll digitally if they don't have physical dice, or if someone wishes to test their luck.
